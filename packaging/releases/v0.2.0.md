# Inside Battery 0.2.0 — development preview

Apple Silicon (arm64), macOS 13 or newer. Intel binaries are not included.

- Native-style compact battery menu, slimmer battery shapes and lighter High Power purple.
- Grey Energy Mode outlines, centred High Power arrows and compact menu-bar sizing.
- Optional macOS time-until-full estimate; charger capacity clearly labelled in watts.
- Energy Mode mouse clicks keep the menu open and no longer dim during switching.
- One real app, visible Quit, and no Disable Quick Power Switching item after enablement.

## Installation

Download `InsideBattery-0.2.0-arm64.zip`, extract it, and move the complete
`Inside Battery.app` to Applications before opening it. Avoid running multiple
copies of the app. Verify the archive against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Preview limits

This development archive is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized.
Gatekeeper may prevent opening a downloaded copy. No quarantine bypass is supplied.
It is not an official Homebrew Cask or MacPorts release.

Power switching is optional and requires administrator approval. The development
Mac's original helper works with a temporary absolute-path launch, but normal
Service Management registration failed to resolve the bundle-relative executable.
Fresh-install approval, switching and restart persistence for this newly
source-built helper pair are unverified. The diagnostic recovery is not a public
installer. Do not treat this preview as a stable power-switching release.

The significant-energy list uses a private macOS 26.2 integration and may report
unavailable. Charger capacity is a charger rating, not live charging watts.

Verification: 168 executable self-checks, client/helper signature pinning, complete
bundle verification, and ZIP extraction round-trip. Desktop install testing and
notarization are separate requirements for a stable release.
